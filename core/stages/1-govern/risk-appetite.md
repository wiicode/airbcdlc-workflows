# Stage: risk-appetite

Write (or confirm) the top-level harms and their tolerances. These are the roots of the tree; everything backs out to them.

## Steps
1. Confirm or replace the default harm roots (`core/steering/risk-core/harms.md`).
2. For each, draft one or two tolerance sentences in the signable form: "The company will / will not carry <exposure> beyond <threshold>."
3. Route each tolerance to the executive who signs it (`authorized` by a human).
